Key Observations
🏆
Women convert, Men build scale
Women delivered 4 of 6 purchases and $1,231.15 at a 4.00x ROAS. Men reached twice as many people (28,853) and drove 71 of 97 app installs, but returned only 0.90x.
🇰🇼
Kuwait is the only market converting
Every purchase came from Kuwait, at a 3.34x ROAS. Bahrain and Qatar took $256.68 (35% of spend) and produced 9 adds to cart, 35 installs and no purchases.
🎠
Men's carousel drives installs, video drives sales
The Ceramic Brown Carousel produced 64 of the Men ad set's 71 installs and all 16 of its adds to cart at a 1.12% CTR. Both Men purchases came from the Video Ad, despite its 0.43% CTR.
🛒
Checkout is converting well
Of 32 adds to cart, 7 reached checkout and 6 purchased. The bigger drop is earlier: only 4.7% of 674 content views became an add to cart.
👥
25 to 34 is the core audience
The 25 to 34 group is 49.6% of reach and produced 4 of 6 purchases. Women 35 to 44 added the other 2 purchases from just 3,931 people reached.
🔁
Frequency is building quickly
Campaign frequency reached 3.87 in 12 days, with Men in Bahrain at 3.79. Expect CTR to soften within the next two weeks unless new creatives are added.
How to Read This Report
  1. Results are adds to cart. The campaign optimises for Add to Cart, so Meta's "Results" column equals adds to cart. Purchases (6) include 1 in-app purchase; all ad sets target iOS on Instagram.
  2. Meta does not return conversions with an area breakdown. Adds to cart, installs and purchases come back as "not available" by area, and so do their costs. Unlike Nabat's messaging campaigns there is no cost metric to recover them from, so area efficiency is ranked on cost per link click, which Meta measures directly.
  3. Country reach sums to 43,189 against a campaign reach of 37,820. Meta de-duplicates unique reach at campaign level; country and area figures are summed across the Men and Women ad sets.
  4. 2 adds to cart are attributed to India with no spend or impressions there, likely users who saw the ad in the GCC and converted later while in India. They are included in the campaign total of 32 but not in any country tab.
  5. The campaign is named KWT but targets Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar. If Kuwait only was intended, the location targeting needs updating.
  6. First reporting period. The campaign started on 30 October 2026, so there are no month-on-month comparisons yet. Figures use the campaign's default attribution setting.
